## Working principles
Investigate only systems and data you own or are explicitly authorised to examine. Preserve originals, analyse verified working copies, record hashes and UTC timestamps, distinguish facts from hypotheses, and never publish private or confidential evidence.

Course availability, names, pricing and subscription inclusion can change. Verify official Coursera pages and your checkout price. Course certificates are learning credentials, not a job guarantee or a substitute for independently proctored industry certifications.
