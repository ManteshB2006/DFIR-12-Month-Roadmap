# Start Here — Your DFIR Learning System

Welcome. This repository is a 12-month structured plan for a complete beginner targeting junior DFIR, incident response or SOC roles.

## Your routine
Study target: 3 hours/day, 6 days/week.
- 45–60 minutes: course learning
- 60–75 minutes: guided lab
- 30–45 minutes: portfolio investigation
- 15–30 minutes: notes, review and interview practice

## Follow in order
1. [12-month roadmap](ROADMAP.md)
2. [Month 01](month-01/README.md), then month-02 through month-12
3. [Certificate tracker](CERTIFICATE-TRACKER.md)
4. [Project tracker](PROJECT-TRACKER.md)
5. Copy [project plan](templates/PROJECT-PLAN.md) and [investigation report](templates/INVESTIGATION-REPORT.md) for every case
6. Record evidence in [evidence log](templates/EVIDENCE-LOG.csv) and custody form
7. Complete a [weekly review](templates/WEEKLY-REVIEW.md)

## Final outcome
Aim for 8–10 polished, evidence-backed investigations and one capstone. A certificate is a learning milestone, not proof of professional experience. Be transparent that lab projects are simulated.

## Legal and safety boundary
Use only your own lab, explicit written authorisation, or lawful training datasets. Do not scan or investigate third-party systems without permission. Never publish credentials, private data, confidential evidence or live malware.
