# Job Preparation — Months 11–12

## Entry-level roles to target
- Junior SOC Analyst
- Security Operations Analyst
- Junior Incident Response Analyst
- Cybersecurity Analyst
- Junior DFIR Analyst
- Digital Forensics Investigator where requirements fit

## Resume evidence
For each project describe context, evidence type, tool, method, verifiable result and report link. Label lab work as simulated; do not claim production experience you do not have.

## Technical interview topics
- Evidence acquisition, hashing and chain of custody
- NTFS artefacts, Windows Event Logs and Registry
- TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP and PCAP analysis
- Volatile versus non-volatile evidence
- Memory-analysis concepts and Volatility 3
- Incident response lifecycle and containment decisions
- Timeline construction, UTC and clock skew
- IOC versus behavioural detection
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping and confidence
- Writing executive summaries for technical and non-technical readers

## Weekly job-search routine
- Review five relevant job descriptions.
- Map recurring skills to evidence in your portfolio.
- Maintain a SOC/IR resume and a DFIR-focused resume.
- Practise two technical questions.
- Apply where eligibility and experience requirements are realistic.
- Keep personal contact details and application records out of this public repository.
