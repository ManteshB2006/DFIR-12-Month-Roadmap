# Weekly DFIR Study Template

Use this six-day schedule alongside the monthly plan. Each study day is approximately three hours.

## Daily structure
| Block | Duration | Activity |
|---|---:|---|
| Concepts | 60 min | Study one focused topic and write notes |
| Practical work | 90 min | Complete a lab or investigate a dataset |
| Documentation | 30 min | Record commands, observations, hashes, and questions |

## Example week
| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learn the core concept | One-page notes |
| 2 | Learn the relevant tool | Tool/command reference |
| 3 | Guided lab | Lab notes and screenshots |
| 4 | Repeat independently | Independent findings |
| 5 | Investigate a small scenario | Mini timeline |
| 6 | Review and document | Short report |
| 7 | Rest or optional light review | No required deliverable |

## Good habits
- Keep raw evidence separate from working copies.
- Record time zones and timestamp formats.
- Hash evidence where appropriate.
- Capture enough detail for another person to reproduce your steps.
- Do not present a hypothesis as a proven fact.
