# Ethical DFIR Lab Rules

1. Investigate only systems you own or have explicit written authorisation to examine.
2. Use isolated virtual machines and lawful public training datasets.
3. Do not access, scan, disrupt or collect data from third-party systems without permission.
4. Never upload personal data, credentials, private keys, confidential employer evidence or customer data.
5. Do not commit live malware or weaponised payloads. Use hashes, metadata and safe reports.
6. Preserve original evidence; analyse verified working copies.
7. Record acquisition details, hashes, timestamps, tool versions and evidence transfers.
8. Use UTC consistently and document clock drift/timezone assumptions.
9. Redact sensitive identifiers where disclosure is unnecessary.
10. Do not claim attribution or compromise unless evidence supports it.
11. Respect dataset licences and platform rules.
12. Stop if authorisation or scope is unclear.

This repository is educational, not legal advice or an operational incident-response authority.
